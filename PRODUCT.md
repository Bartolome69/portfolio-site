# Product: Bart Etcheverry Portfolio

## What it is
Personal portfolio website for Bart Etcheverry — delivery lead at Planes (a London product studio), independent builder of apps and websites.

## Users
Potential freelance clients, collaborators, recruiters, and people Bart meets at events or online who want to learn more about his work.

## Brand & tone
Calm, text-led, structured. Inspired by brianlovin.com — clean, indie, slightly polished. No agency-speak, no hero sections, no testimonials. The site should feel like a well-organized personal document, not a marketing page.

## Anti-references
- Giant hero headlines with CTA buttons
- Animated gradients or floating shapes
- Client logo walls
- "Passionate about creating amazing experiences"
- Contact forms (mailto only)
- Dark mode toggle
- Stock photography

## Strategic principles
- Content-first: every pixel serves the words
- One page, navigable by section anchors
- Shippable as-is — no placeholder content
- Cookie-free, no analytics consent needed
